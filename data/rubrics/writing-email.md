Our best guess at how a rater scores this task. This is NOT the official ETS rubric. Official guides: https://www.es.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/writing-rubrics.pdf.

Task: Write an Email. Score 0 to 5. Judge two things: does the email do its job, and is the language accurate and polite enough for the reader.

5: The email covers every point the prompt asks for and gives useful detail. Tone fits the reader. Sentences vary. Word choice is precise. Slips are rare and small.
4: The email covers the points and is easy to follow. Tone is mostly right. Detail is enough. A few errors appear, none of which block meaning.
3: The email covers most points, but some parts are thin, vague or off topic. Language range is modest. Several errors show in grammar, word form or tone. Some lines need a second read.
2: The email tries the task but fails to deliver. Points are missing or barely developed. Sentences are simple or tangled. Errors pile up and hide the message in places.
1: Very little content. Language is broken or copied from the prompt. Errors are serious and constant.
0: Blank, not English, off topic, copied wholesale from the prompt, or random keys.
