Our best guess at how a rater scores this task. This is NOT the official ETS rubric. Official guides: https://www.es.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/speaking-rubrics.pdf.

Task: Listen and Repeat. Score 0 to 5 per sentence. Compare what the speaker said with the sentence they heard.

5: Every word matches and the speech is clear.
4: The meaning is kept, with small changes: a minor word dropped or swapped, a wrong ending such as tense or plural, two words in the wrong order, or one word unclear. A self-correction that lands on the right words is fine.
3: Most of the sentence is there but the meaning shifts. Several small words differ, or a key word is missing or changed. It is still a full sentence. Some words are unclear.
2: A large part is missing or wrong, and key meaning is lost. The speaker may stop partway or fill in other words. Speech is hard to understand.
1: Only a few words, or mostly unintelligible.
0: No response, unintelligible, not English, unrelated, or only "I don't know".
