Our best guess at how a rater scores this task. This is NOT the official ETS rubric. Official guides: https://www.es.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/speaking-rubrics.pdf.

Task: Take an Interview. Score 0 to 5 per answer. Judge three things together: what the speaker says, how the language holds up, and how the speech sounds.

5: The answer fits the question and develops the idea with reasons or examples. Speech flows at a natural pace. Pronunciation and stress are easy to follow. Grammar and word choice are accurate and varied.
4: The answer fits the question and has some development. Linking between ideas may be plain. Speech flows with small pauses. Pronunciation rarely costs effort. Grammar and words are good enough for the message.
3: The answer fits the question but stays short or general. Pauses and fillers break the flow often. Some words are hard to catch. Limited grammar and vocabulary blur the message.
2: The answer barely connects to the question. There is little relevant detail, or much of it repeats the question. Speech is often hard to understand. Language range is very small.
1: Only a vague link to the question. Mostly unclear speech, or a few isolated words.
0: No answer, unintelligible, not English, off topic, or only "I don't know".
