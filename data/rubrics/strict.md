STRICT MODE. These rules apply the same guides more strictly. They override the "no error-count limits" rule in calibration.md, for strict grades only. The goal is a practice score that is harder to earn than the real one.

S1. Borderline always drops. If the response sits between two levels, give the LOWER whole level. Give a half point only when you can quote evidence for the higher level in every descriptor line.
S2. A 5 is rare. Give 5 only when you can find NO non-typo error and every descriptor line of level 5 is met with quoted evidence.
S3. Error caps (count only quoted, non-typo errors in grammar, word form, word choice, idiom, register or social convention):
    - 1 error: at most 4.5.
    - 2 or 3 errors: at most 4.
    - 4 or 5 errors: at most 3.5.
    - 6 or more errors: at most 3.
    Two or more typos in one response count as one error.
S4. Count every non-standard register form as a full error, even if it is common in some regions: "Sir/Ma'am", "request you to", "kindly", "revert", "post 11am", "upto", "timings", "stands cancelled", "off late", "Yours Sincerely".
S5. Development (Writing and Interview): an answer with no specific example, number, name or personal detail is at most 3. An answer that only repeats ideas from the prompt or from the other students is at most 3.
S6. Email: every requested point must be covered clearly. One missing or vague point means at most 3.
S7. Range: if most sentences share one simple pattern ("I think... because..."), or the same word repeats as the main idea word 3 or more times, the score is at most 4.
S8. Interview delivery (audio only): each filler ("uh", "um", "so", "kind of", "like") after the first two lowers delivery. A pause over 2 seconds, a false start or a restart counts as a pace fault. 3 or more pace faults means delivery is at most 3. An answer that stops well before the time ends, or is cut off mid-idea, is at most 3.5.
S9. Listen and Repeat: 5 only for word-for-word, including contractions, plurals and articles ("want to" said as "wanna" is a change). One changed or missing word of any kind is at most 4. Two or more changes is at most 3.
S10. Every cap you apply must be named in "deductions" with the rule number, for example "S3: 3 errors, cap 4".
