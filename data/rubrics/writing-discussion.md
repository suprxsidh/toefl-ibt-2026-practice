Our best guess at how a rater scores this task. This is NOT the official ETS rubric. Official guides: https://www.es.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/writing-rubrics.pdf.

Task: Write for an Academic Discussion. Score 0 to 5. Judge whether the post adds a real point to the thread, and whether the language carries it.

5: The post answers the question and adds its own view with a reason and an example or detail. The ideas are clear on first read. Sentences vary. Word choice is precise. Slips are rare and small.
4: The post answers the question and gives a reason with some support. Ideas are easy to follow. A few errors appear, none of which block meaning.
3: The post mostly answers the question. Support is partial: a reason or example may be vague, missing or off topic. Several errors show in grammar, word form or idiom.
2: The post tries to join the thread, but ideas stay thin or only partly relevant. Language is limited. Errors pile up and make the ideas hard to follow.
1: Few clear ideas. Language is broken, or most words come from the posts above. Errors are serious and constant.
0: Blank, not English, off topic, copied from the prompt, or random keys.
