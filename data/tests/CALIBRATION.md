# Calibration notes: official practice items vs test3

The public ETS practice items looked easy to us. Real upper modules are harder. test3 is set above the practice level. Target: a strong taker scores 75-85% on the upper module.

## Measured (ETS, 4 reading and 4 listening modules)

| Measure | ETS practice | test3 upper |
|---|---|---|
| Academic passage (words) | 168-201 | 206 |
| Daily text (words) | 32-139 | 72-136 |
| Complete the Words text (words) | 74-86 | 79-94 |
| Blank word length (letters) | about 5.5, common words | 8.1, content words |
| Questions per task | 2-3 daily, 5 academic | same |
| Option length (words) | 5.7 | 7.7 |
| Key words found in passage (stems) | 66% | 32% |
| Conversation (words) | 57-91 | 109-176 |
| Announcement (words) | 42-72 | 84-130 |
| Talk (words) | 162-230 | 227-255 |
| Choose prompt (words) | about 8 | 9 |
| Listen and Repeat (words) | 5-18 | 8-23 |
| Build a Sentence answer (words) | 5-12 | 12-15 |

## Question mix (ETS reading, 40 items)

Detail 40%, inference 15%, purpose or main idea 15%, rhetorical or paragraph relation 15%, vocabulary 10%, EXCEPT 5%. Listening: detail about 45%, topic or purpose 20%, why-mention 15%, next step, implication or idiom function 20%.

## How ETS builds wrong options

Out of scope or unrelated 30%. True but not asked 35%. Reversed 15%. Reuses passage words but wrong claim 20%. The key often copies passage phrases. The key is rarely the longest.

## Rules for test3 upper

1. At least 60% inference, purpose, rhetorical or vocabulary items. Detail items need a paraphrase.
2. Key shares under 35% of content words with the passage. Distractors share more.
3. Every distractor is true-but-not-asked, reversed, over-claimed or a later detail.
4. Blanks are content words of 7+ letters, with some suffix traps.
5. Key is never the unique longest. Keep keys within 5 characters of the longest in about a third of items.
6. Talks of 220+ words, with idioms and a hedge ("That said").
7. Speaking and writing: sentences at the top of the ETS range or longer.

Speech speed: no ETS audio supplied. Text only.
